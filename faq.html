Frequently Asked Questions

Questions finance professionals ask before we start.

About the coaching, the programmes, and how it works. If your question isn't here, email me directly at hello@celinagoodfellowconsultancy.com.

Who is this coaching for?

Finance professionals, from Analyst to MD, across Investment Banking, Corporate Finance, Private Equity, FP&A and Capital Markets, and the specialist roles that work alongside them, such as compliance, legal and risk. It is for people whose expertise is not in question, but whose communication decides whether they are heard, trusted and promoted.

Is executive communication coaching worth it?

In finance, senior people judge your analysis by how you deliver it. A strong recommendation that is buried, hedged or lost under pushback costs you credibility in the rooms where decisions and promotions are made.

Coaching closes that gap quickly because it works on your real situations. Clients describe the change in how they influence senior discussions as clear and immediate, and some notice it after just three sessions. You can read what they say on the What Clients Say page.

How is this different from presentation training or my company's communication training?

Most communication training is generic. Several clients have described firm-wide sessions, delivered in person or online to groups of 10 to 60 people, focused on counting ums and ahs and on general techniques like eye contact and body language. Useful to know. But very little of it survives the next difficult meeting.

My coaching works the other way round. It is 1:1 and built entirely around you. Every session starts from a real situation in your work: the board paper, the investment committee, the call where you know the pushback is coming. We don't rehearse generic techniques. We change the specific patterns that show up when you are under pressure, so that when it matters, you hold the room.

I wrote about this in Why the best communication training in finance teaches you nothing you can use.

What happens in the sessions?

Every session is built around a real situation in your work, whether it's coming up or happening right now: a board presentation, an investment committee, an investor call or a difficult senior conversation. Between sessions, you complete a practice task and receive written feedback.

We work on handling pushback, leading with your judgement and staying composed under scrutiny, along with the specific moments you need to get right in your role. Your Personal Communication Playbook captures everything we build together, and it is yours to keep.

Which programme is right for me?

The 5-Week Boardroom Sprint is a focused start: five weeks on the challenge that matters most right now, or an event you need to be ready for. The 10-Week Boardroom Authority Programme is for lasting change across every room, not just the next one. The Delivery Gap is a self-study programme for analysts and associates. The Interview Intensive is a single 90-minute session for one interview coming up soon.

If you are not sure, the free Voice Audit is the place to start. I will recommend the right next step.

Can you help me prepare for a finance interview?

Yes. Many clients come to me after an interview where their experience didn't come across: the CV got them into the room, but their answers didn't do it justice. If you have interviews coming up for an Associate, VP, Director or MD role, the Boardroom Sprint can be built around them, using the questions you are likely to face. For one interview coming up soon, the 90-minute Interview Intensive is designed for exactly that.

What if I get interrupted or talked over in senior meetings?

This is one of the most common things clients bring to me. You know what you want to say, but by the time there is a gap, the conversation has moved on, or someone more senior finishes your point for you. It isn't a confidence problem. It is about when you come in and how you open. We work on entering the conversation cleanly and making your first sentence carry your view, so the room lets you finish.

I'm a technical specialist in compliance, legal or risk. Is this for me?

Yes. Specialists often have the hardest translation job in the room. Your expertise is why you are there, but the people making the decision need to hear what it means for the business, not the technical route to it. When that translation doesn't happen, specialists get left out of the meetings where decisions are made. We work on leading with what your expertise means for the decision, in language the business can act on.

English isn't my first language. Is this the right coaching for me?

Yes. I work with native and non-native English speakers, and the challenges are the same. When a point isn't landing, it is almost never a language issue. It's structure: what you lead with, what you leave out, and how you hold your position. That is what we work on.

Is the coaching online?

Yes. All sessions are online, by video call, which is why I work with finance professionals across Europe, the GCC, Asia Pacific and North America.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Most clients notice a difference within the first couple of weeks. Because each session is built on what is happening in your work, you put it into practice straight away, in real meetings.
