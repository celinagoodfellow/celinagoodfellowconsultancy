The Problem

The feedback no one gives you.

Directors and MDs rarely tell you that you hedged before your recommendation, conceded a point you didn't need to, or spent three minutes on context before reaching your conclusion.

They simply form a view of your seniority. And that view shapes what comes next in your career.

The Delivery Gap gives you the perspective those conversations leave out. It was developed from 1:1 coaching with analysts and associates across investment banking, corporate finance and private equity, and it targets the specific delivery patterns that read as junior.

The gap is not about confidence. It is about structure.

Inside the Programme

Four modules. Tested in real deal rooms.

The analysts who hold the room lead with their recommendation, sequence their reasoning in the right order, and respond to challenge without losing their position. These are learnable skills — and every task uses your real work.

Module 1

Why You Sound Junior (Even When You're Not)

The four-part Judgment–Why–Risk–Next Step framework, and the patterns that separate analysts who lose the room from those who hold it.

Module 2

The 30-Second Structure

A delivery sequence that gets your recommendation across in the first half-minute. Seniority comes from order and clarity, not volume.

Module 3

Stop Sounding Uncertain

The hedging words that signal junior — ‘just’, ‘I think’, ‘maybe’ — identified, and replaced with language that holds.

Module 4

What to Say When You're Put on the Spot

A four-step framework for handling pushback from directors and MDs, so you keep the ground you have already earned.
