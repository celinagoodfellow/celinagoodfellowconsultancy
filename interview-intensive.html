Who It's For

When the interview is in the diary.

Whether it's a step up, a move to a new firm or an internal promotion, your CV has done its job. Now your answers need to do the same: your experience landing clearly, your judgement coming through in the first sentence, and your composure holding when the questions get harder.

How It Works

One focused session, built around your role.

  • Before the session, you send me the role and the job description.
  • We work on how you open, the questions you are most likely to face, and how your experience comes across in two sentences, not ten.
  • The session is recorded, and you receive written notes afterwards.
